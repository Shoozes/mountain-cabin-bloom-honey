## Drawing decisions

The former bright two-pixel crossing at every horizontal gravel seam created a
regular landmark. Quiet ground replaces that crossing. Interior aggregates use
unequal angular footprints, connected light/shadow planes, sparse highlights and
different gap patterns. The 16px checkpoint was reviewed at native size, 6× repeat
and 4× connected views before the 64px pass.

Finite authored compositions still recur. Whole-feature masking can leave narrow
terrain or paths quieter than broad surfaces; this is part of the current drawing
contract. Structural checks and artist review do not establish owner acceptance.
Artwork remains **unapproved** until the owner reviews the source-bound candidate.
